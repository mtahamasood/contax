- (direction by user) User asked coach to propose and finalize the answers for the change-order / decision-recording governance problem
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - separation of powers: any staff can RAISE a change; nobody who raises it can RATIFY it; recording-as-approved is a distinct gated step
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - change order lifecycle states: Draft -> Proposed(cost+schedule delta mandatory) -> Client-Aware -> Approved -> Executed -> Billed; each transition stamped with who/when/evidence, append-only
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - three-tier rupee thresholds (company-configurable, defaults): Tier A <=1% of contract or Rs50k = site engineer proposes + client negative-confirm; Tier B up to 5% or Rs500k = requires manager sign-off AND client evidence; Tier C above = owner/admin sign-off AND explicit client approval, no negative-confirm
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - client ratification is mandatory for money: negative confirmation (silence=accept over N days) allowed ONLY for Tier A; Tier B/C need positive client evidence (voice note/screenshot/in-app tap) attached before Approved
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - segregation of duties: the person who logs a purchase/consumption against a change order cannot be the same person who proposed it (enforced by role, cross-rank collusion required to defeat)
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - no retroactive change orders: a CO must reach Approved BEFORE its linked costs are billable; work done first can be logged but is flagged 'executed before approval' and cannot be billed to client until ratified - kills the end-of-project surprise bill
- (decision by coach) FINALIZED - fraud surveillance on COs: rate-vs-benchmark check, CO frequency/velocity per engineer flagged cross-project, round-number bias, 'just-under-threshold' clustering detection (COs suspiciously priced at 99% of a sign-off tier)
