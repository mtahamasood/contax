- (idea by coach) Architecture note: build client surface as responsive web app first, wrap as installable app (PWA/shell) later - one codebase serves both the link-tappers and the installers; report link and app show the same live view
- (decision by user) WhatsApp delivery is a PDF report, NOT a link to the app - no app link sent on WhatsApp
- (decision by user) MVP target is a responsive web app; backend must be architected (API-first) so future mobile apps can be built against it easily
- (idea by coach) Weekly PDFs archived server-side as a numbered series = tamper-evident record; client's own copy matches the archive, and a missing week is visible
- (insight by coach) PDF is forwardable: overseas client's family in Pakistan sees it, and it travels to other prospective clients as organic marketing - so the PDF must be branded and self-explanatory, but must not leak margins/supplier rates
- (decision by user) Approval/change-order mechanism happens OUTSIDE the app (WhatsApp voice notes, texts, calls) - cultural read of the vertical; the app records approvals after the fact for later reference, surfaced in the weekly WhatsApp report or in-app for app clients
- (decision by user) Maintain relevant project documents in the app: receipts, architectural drawings etc
- (insight by coach) RISK: a decision recorded after the fact is written unilaterally by the party who benefits from it - adds nothing in a dispute unless the client confirms or the client's own evidence is attached
- (idea by coach) Negative confirmation model: 'Decisions recorded this week' section in report/app; silence for a stated window = accepted, one tap or one WhatsApp reply to dispute - culturally light, produces a bilateral record (same pattern as bank statements)
- (idea by coach) Attach the client's own evidence to the decision record: forward the client's voice note/screenshot into the entry so the record is the client's own voice, not the company's paraphrase
- (idea by coach) Every change record must carry cost delta and schedule delta at logging time - disputes are never 'I didn't choose marble', they're 'I didn't know it would cost this much'
- (idea by coach) Drawings need version control and an 'issued for construction' marker - building from a superseded drawing is a classic dispute; receipts belong linked to ledger entries, not in a generic folder
- (idea by coach) Weekly report should state 'decisions recorded this week: none' when empty - makes unrecorded verbal decisions visible by their absence
