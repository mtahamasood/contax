02Structure over behaviour

Engineering trust into the frame

Rather than asking people to behave honestly, the session designed structures that make dishonesty require collusion — and left a trail when attempted.

Coach

Role-separated double entry

Storekeeper logs arrival, engineer logs consumption, accounts logs payment; the system auto-reconciles. Lying now requires cross-rank collusion.

Coach

Evidence-mandatory entries

Geotagged challan + truck photo required; OCR reads the challan so quantities come off paper, not a typist.

Coach

Append-only ledger

Corrections leave a visible trail; late or backdated entries auto-flag.

Coach

Day-one rule engine

Duplicate invoice numbers, round-number bias, rate-vs-market feed, consumption-vs-BOQ thresholds — no ML required.

Coach

Fraud in three generations

Rule engine on day one → cross-project math in month one → learned ML anomaly models in year one. No need to wait for a baseline.

You

Even fraudulent data has value

With data, ML/AI can flag and investigate fraud — impossible with no data at all. Check actuals against industry-average labour productivity and material consumption.

The benchmark moat, stacked

03Technique · SCAMPER

Seven lenses on the frame

Each lens tilted the design. Substitute, Eliminate, Combine and Reverse carried the heaviest load — the others sharpened decisions already in motion.

S
Substituteswap a part for another
  • 3-tier client delivery: T1 app for the willing, T2 weekly WhatsApp PDF, T3 provisioned WhatsApp integration.
  • Substitute a live dashboard with a weekly PDF push for clients who won't install.
  • OCR reads the challan — substitute the typist with the paper itself.
  • Supplier confirmation swapped for ledger reconciliation (no supplier adoption needed).
C
Combinemerge events
  • One capture, four pillars: challan photo → OCR → material stock + accounts payable* + rule-engine flag.
  • Site photo = trust evidence + %-completion proof + fraud datapoint in one upload.
  • One weekly LLM pass writes narrative + “decisions this week” + negative-confirmation prompts.
  • Three benchmark sources collapse into one “what should X cost?” service.
  • *Correction: the payable is a recommendation the accountant must edit & sign off — human-in-the-loop on money.
A
Adaptborrow a pattern
  • Adapt WhatsApp-native capture — absorb WhatsApp, don't fight it.
  • Negative confirmation borrowed from bank statements: silence over N days = accepted.
M
Modify / Magnifytune the dials
  • Tiered rupee thresholds (A/B/C), all company-configurable.
  • Magnify anomaly reach: flag under-consumption, not just overspend.
P
Put to other userepurpose the asset
  • The pooled benchmark dataset becomes a data product / revenue line, not just a detection aid.
  • The client acts as a free auditor — visibility is zero-cost fraud detection.
E
Eliminatestrip the MVP
  • Kill the client login → phone identity + one-time OTP link.
  • Kill manual %-completion → compute it from BOQ line items marked done.
  • Defer drawing/quality/inspection pillars; drop the ML engine from MVP.
  • Replace free-text entry with scans wherever possible.
  • Overrides: keep versioned drawings in v1; keep both email & phone as identity.
R
Reverseflip the direction
  • Reverse the burden of proof: the company proactively proves innocence each week.
  • Reverse anomaly direction — suspiciously-clean numbers are a flag too.
  • Reverse who enters data (client logs observations) — later rejected in the PK context.

Meta-insight from the Combine lens: the product has ~3 atomic capture events — material-in via challan, money movement, site photo — and everything else is a view computed from them. Design the capture layer once; project it into every surface.

04The fake-change-order fraud vector

Change-order governance, finalized

You asked the coach to propose and finalize the governance answer. The result: a separation-of-powers lifecycle where nobody who raises a change may ratify it.

Draft→ Proposed Δcost+Δschedule→ Client-Aware→ Approved→ Executed→ Billed
Tier A ≤1% or Rs 50k

Site engineer proposes + client negative-confirm (silence = accept).

Tier B ≤5% or Rs 500k

Manager sign-off and positive client evidence before Approved.

Tier C above

Owner/admin sign-off and explicit client approval — no negative-confirm.

  • Separation of powers. Any staff can raise a change; nobody who raises it can ratify it — recording-as-approved is a distinct gated step.
  • No retroactive change orders. A CO must reach Approved before its costs are billable. Work done first is logged but flagged “executed before approval” — killing the end-of-project surprise bill.
  • Segregation of duties. Whoever logs a purchase against a CO cannot be whoever proposed it.
  • Immutable audit trail. Append-only; edits create new versions, originals preserved; no deletion, only a “cancelled” state.
  • Fraud surveillance on COs. Rate-vs-benchmark, CO velocity per engineer, round-number bias, and “just-under-threshold” clustering at 99% of a sign-off tier.
  • The client always sees a CO before it is billed, never after. Every CO auto-appears in the weekly PDF with its state and rupee delta; “decisions this week: none” when empty.
North Star — your standing meta-requirement

(a) Prefer a configurable choice with a recommended pre-filled default over a hard-coded single answer. (b) Keep internal-approval and client-facing concerns as two separate axes. For future queries the coach decides using this north star, then notifies you for review rather than asking. The Tier A/B/C table became recommended defaults on both axes — every cell company-overridable.

08A principle that surfaced

Data credibility as a first-class score

Reinstating supplier reconciliation produced a generalizable principle: any fact confirmed by two independent captures carries higher confidence — credibility becomes a scored attribute, not an afterthought.

SOURCE A · primary, real-time

OCR'd delivery challan

Captured at material-in. Remains the primary real-time control against fake or inflated purchases.

⇄reconcile
SOURCE B · secondary, periodic

Supplier's account statement

The customer-account khata issued to the company (not the supplier's private profit ledger). Periodically OCR'd and matched against accumulated Source A.

Result

Agreement → confidence

Two independent sources agreeing raises a data-credibility score — a credibility signal on the data, not only fraud-catching.

Result

Divergence → investigation

Disagreement raises an internal-first anomaly investigation. The pattern extends beyond materials: photo + ledger, milestone + consumption.

Parked 03

Suppliers pay for placement / verified badges / leads; consumption data routes the right supplier to the right project.

Parked 04

Parked 06

Companies pay to earn and display a rating that wins hesitant clients — monetizing trust itself.

10Session synthesis

What was built

Client dashboard (3 pillars) ⟶ two-sided anti-fraud trust instrument for construction companies

Contax started as a client dashboard and converged into a two-sided anti-fraud trust instrument for construction companies — the buyer, whose interests are primary.

Spine discovery: the automated weekly proof-of-innocence report is the wedge that back-solves the whole MVP by forcing three atomic capture events — OCR challan, money movement, site photo. Everything else is a computed view.

How trust is engineered: separation of powers + human-in-loop sign-offs + append-only audit + source-agreement data-credibility scoring — not by forcing behaviour change. Users stay on WhatsApp; the structure does the work.

The moat & the future: the benchmark stack (govt rate books → company history → cross-company pool) enables cold-start ML anomaly detection in v1 and is the long-term moat plus the parked-monetization base. AI/ML is a standing lens across three execution locations — on-device, self-hosted SLM, and third-party API behind redaction.
